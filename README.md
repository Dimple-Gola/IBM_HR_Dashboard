# IBM_HR_Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, and potential retention drivers using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset.

The project transforms raw HR data into an interactive business intelligence solution that helps HR teams understand:

- Overall workforce composition
- Employee attrition patterns
- Departments and roles with higher attrition
- The relationship between overtime and attrition
- Attrition across age and tenure groups
- Job satisfaction and work-life balance patterns

🎯 Business Problem

Employee attrition can negatively impact organizations through:

-Increased recruitment costs
-Loss of experienced employees
-Reduced productivity
-Increased workload for remaining employees
-Additional training and onboarding requirements

HR teams need to understand where attrition is concentrated and which employee characteristics are associated with higher turnover.

This project analyzes employee-level HR data to identify workforce trends and potential attrition risk patterns.

❓ Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:

1. What is the overall employee attrition rate?
2. Which departments have the highest attrition rates?
3. Which job roles experience higher attrition?
4. Does overtime correlate with higher employee attrition?
5. Which age groups have the highest attrition?
6. Which tenure groups are most affected by attrition?
7. Is job satisfaction associated with employee attrition?
8. How does work-life balance relate to attrition?
9. How does attrition vary across department and gender?
